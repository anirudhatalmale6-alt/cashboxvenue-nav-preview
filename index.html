What changes

  • Below 900px a menu button appears and opens a slide-in panel with all five links — Venues, Talent, Events, The Wall, Contact. Right now those links are set to display:none on phones and tablets with nothing to replace them.
  • The button is a real <button>, so it works with a keyboard. Tab to it, press Enter. Escape closes the panel, and focus returns to the button.
  • Background scrolling is locked while the panel is open.
  • Desktop is untouched — same bar, same spacing, same hover underline.
  • The logo keeps its real proportions instead of being squashed, and the file went from 2.2 MB to 40 KB.

One thing to decide — the header logo

The image in the header is a photograph, 1679×937 and 2.2 MB, shown at about 70 pixels tall. At that size the words "Cash Box Venue" printed inside it are too small to read, so it reads as a small photo rather than a logo. Your own sign-in and dashboard pages already use a text lockup instead. Three options — your call, they all cost the same:

A — what is there now Keep the photo. I would still compress it — 2.2 MB down to 40 KB with no visible difference. The wordmark stays unreadable at header size.
B — the lockup your portal already uses (my suggestion) CASHBOX●VENUE Legible at any size, sharp on every screen, weighs nothing, and matches your sign-in and dashboard pages so the site stops looking like two different products.
C — both CASHBOX●VENUE A small square crop of the cash box as an icon, with the readable text beside it. Keeps the character of the artwork and stays legible.
